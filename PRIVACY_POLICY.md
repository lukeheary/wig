# WIG Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 20, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 20, 2026

Who Is Going ("WIG," "we," "us," or "our") provides the WIG service. This
Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and retain information
when you use the WIG mobile application, the website at
[whoisgoing.com](https://www.whoisgoing.com), and related services
(collectively, the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide

We may collect information that you provide directly, including:

- **Account and profile information:** email address, username, first and last
  name, profile photo, city, state, gender, and date of birth.
- **Authentication information:** information needed to create and authenticate
  your account. If you sign in with Apple, Google, or another authentication
  provider, we receive information that provider makes available to us, such as
  your provider account identifier, name, and email address. Your password is
  handled by our authentication provider and is not stored in WIG's application
  database.
- **Social and activity information:** friend requests and friendships, partner
  relationships, followed or hidden places, notification preferences, RSVPs,
  reasons you choose to provide for not attending an event, reviews, ratings,
  and other interactions with the Service.
- **Content you submit:** profile images, event and place information, event
  cover images, reviews, comments, and other content you create or upload.
- **Support communications:** the subject, message, category, severity, status,
  and related information for requests you send to WIG support.

Some profile details, content, and activity may be visible to other WIG users as
described in Section 4.

### Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, we may collect:

- **Device and technical information:** device type, operating system, app or
  browser version, IP address, diagnostic information, request timestamps, and
  similar server-log information.
- **Usage information:** pages or screens viewed, features used, navigation and
  interaction events, referring pages, and approximate usage times. Our website
  uses Google Analytics for this purpose. The iOS app may generate operational
  logs needed to provide and troubleshoot the Service.
- **Notification information:** an Apple Push Notification service device token,
  notification authorization status, registration dates, and whether a token
  has been revoked. A device token identifies an app installation for delivery
  purposes; it is not your phone number.
- **Approximate location:** WIG may infer a general location from information
  such as your selected city and state or your IP address. WIG does not currently
  request continuous or precise device location through the iOS app.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** the website may use cookies, local
  storage, pixels, and similar technologies for authentication, security,
  preferences, analytics, and Service operation.

### Information from other sources

We may receive information from:

- Apple, Google, Clerk, or another provider you use to sign in;
- other users, such as when they send a friend or partner request, RSVP on behalf
  of a partner where the feature permits it, or include you in Service activity;
- event, venue, organizer, mapping, and ticketing sources, including Google
  Places and publicly available or licensed event listings; and
- service providers that help us detect abuse, secure, host, analyze, and operate
  the Service.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- create, authenticate, maintain, and secure accounts;
- provide profiles, event discovery, place pages, social connections, activity
  feeds, RSVPs, reviews, partner features, and other Service functionality;
- personalize content using information such as your city, followed places,
  relationships, and activity;
- deliver service-related emails, in-app messages, and push notifications;
- process uploads and display user and event images;
- respond to support requests and communicate with you;
- measure use of the Service and improve reliability, performance, features, and
  user experience;
- detect, investigate, and prevent spam, fraud, abuse, security incidents, and
  violations of our terms or community rules;
- moderate content and protect WIG, our users, and others;
- comply with law, enforce agreements, and establish, exercise, or defend legal
  claims; and
- create aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably
  identify you.

We do not use data collected from Apple's platform APIs or permission-protected
resources for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

## 3. When We Disclose Information

We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

### Other users and the public

WIG is a social event-discovery service. Depending on the feature and your
relationships, other users may be able to see your username, name, profile photo,
city and state, friend or partner relationships, event activity, RSVPs, ratings,
reviews, and content you create. Some event, place, organizer, and public-profile
pages may also be accessible on the public website. Do not submit content you do
not want shared with the applicable audience.

### Service providers

We disclose information to vendors that process it for us to provide the
Service. These include, as applicable:

- **Clerk** for account authentication and identity management;
- **Neon** for managed database infrastructure;
- **Cloudflare**, including Cloudflare R2, for image and object storage and
  delivery;
- **Vercel** for website and API hosting and delivery;
- **Google**, including Google Analytics and Google Places, for website analytics
  and place-search functionality; and
- **Apple**, including Apple Push Notification service and Sign in with Apple,
  for platform authentication and notification delivery.

These providers may process device, network, account, usage, content, or other
information as necessary to perform services for us. We require service
providers that receive personal information from us to protect it consistently
with this Privacy Policy and applicable law and to use it only for authorized
purposes.

### Legal, safety, and rights protection

We may disclose information if we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to
comply with law or legal process; respond to valid government requests; protect
the rights, safety, and property of WIG, our users, or others; investigate fraud,
abuse, or security issues; or enforce our agreements.

### Business transfers

Information may be disclosed or transferred as part of a merger, financing,
acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, sale of assets, or similar transaction.
Where required, we will provide notice before personal information becomes
subject to a materially different privacy policy.

### At your direction

We may disclose information when you direct us to do so, request an integration,
or otherwise give consent.

## 4. Social Features and User Content

Your visibility on WIG depends on the feature, the audience, and your
relationships with other users. For example:

- profile and public-page information may be visible to other users or website
  visitors;
- friends or partners may see activity and event-attendance information that is
  not shown to unrelated users;
- event organizers, place managers, or administrators may see information needed
  to operate their features; and
- reviews and event or place content may remain visible to their intended
  audience until removed or until your account is deleted, subject to legal and
  operational exceptions described below.

Other people may copy or reshare information that you make visible to them. WIG
cannot control information after another person has copied or shared it outside
the Service.

## 5. Your Choices and Controls

Depending on the feature, you may:

- review and update profile details in the app;
- add, verify, or remove email addresses through account settings;
- manage friendships, partner relationships, place follows, hidden content, and
  notification-muting preferences;
- change push-notification permissions in iOS Settings;
- change camera, photo-library, and calendar permissions in iOS Settings;
- manage cookie controls through your browser and, where available, website
  controls; and
- delete your WIG account from the app.

Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working, but core
features that do not require that permission will remain available where
practicable.

## 6. Account and Data Deletion

You may initiate permanent account deletion within the WIG app from your profile
or account settings. Account deletion is designed to remove your WIG profile,
personal information, relationships, follows, RSVPs, reviews, notifications,
support records, push tokens, and content you created, together with stored
images associated with that content.

Deletion may not be instantaneous in backups, caches, logs, or systems operated
by service providers. Residual copies are isolated from ordinary use and are
deleted or overwritten according to backup and security cycles. We may retain
limited information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, prevent fraud
or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, protect the Service, or maintain
a record of a privacy request. Information that has been aggregated or
de-identified so that it no longer reasonably identifies you may be retained.

If you used Sign in with Apple or another third-party sign-in provider, you may
also manage WIG's access through that provider's account settings. Deleting your
WIG account does not delete your Apple or Google account.

You may also request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us at
**support@whoisgoing.com**. We may need to verify your identity before completing a
request.

## 7. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide
the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including while
your account remains active. The retention period depends on the type of
information, the feature for which it is used, security and fraud-prevention
needs, applicable limitation periods, and legal or contractual requirements.

When information is no longer needed, we delete, de-identify, or securely isolate
it. For example, account data is generally retained until account deletion,
support and security records may be kept for a reasonable period after a matter
is resolved, and routine logs and backups are retained on rolling schedules.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards
designed to protect personal information. These measures may include encrypted
network transmission, managed authentication, access controls, and separation of
production systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure,
and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your sign-in methods and for
promptly notifying us if you believe your account has been compromised.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 may not
create or use a WIG account. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal
information to WIG, contact us at **support@whoisgoing.com** so we can investigate and,
where appropriate, delete it.

Users may be required to provide a date of birth to support age-appropriate
experiences and legal compliance. A date-of-birth field does not mean that WIG
knowingly permits use by children under 13.

## 10. U.S. State Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and whether the applicable law applies to WIG, you
may have rights to request access to or a copy of personal information; learn
about the categories and sources of information we collect; correct inaccurate
information; delete information; obtain data in a portable format; or appeal our
response to a request. You may also have the right not to receive discriminatory
treatment for exercising a privacy right.

WIG does not sell personal information for money. WIG does not use personal
information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If our practices change,
we will update this policy and provide any legally required opt-out mechanism.

To submit a request, email **support@whoisgoing.com** with the subject line "Privacy
Request." Describe your request and the account email or username involved. We
will verify and respond to the request as required by applicable law. An
authorized agent may submit a request where permitted by law, but we may require
proof of authorization and direct identity verification.

## 11. International Data Transfers

WIG and its providers may process and store information in the United States and
other countries where they operate. Those countries may have data-protection laws
that differ from the laws where you live. Where required, we use appropriate
safeguards for international transfers.

If WIG is offered in a jurisdiction that requires a specific lawful basis for
processing, we process information as needed to perform our agreement with you,
based on our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, with
your consent, or to comply with legal obligations, as applicable. You may
withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that occurred before
withdrawal.

## 12. Third-Party Services and Links

The Service may link to ticketing providers, event organizers, venues, maps,
social networks, and other third-party services. Their privacy practices are
governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy. Review those policies
before providing information to them.
